Howard Plaza Towers West offered both small study rooms and larger spaces for group work. The guide placed them on the right side of the first floor. Its photograph identified P-42 as the largest study room in the building.

The 2023 article said students could reserve group rooms in the Undergraduate Library from a phone or computer. The library also had individual study rooms, printers and desktop computers.

The School of Business library welcomed students from every major. The reporter described it as a quiet space close to the building entrance.

The Stacks provided a quieter place to work among the books inside Founders Library. Louis Stokes Library offered a choice between quiet areas and places where study partners could talk.
