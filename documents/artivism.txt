At the time of the October 2025 report, Sankofa hosted the Artivism jam on Tuesday nights. Howard alumni Joshua Hughes and Isaac Alicea hosted the sessions.

Performers at the Sankofa jam could share poems or songs. A backing band played saxophone, drums, piano and bass.

Hughes dated Artivism's founding to December 2022. The founders described a need for a consistent space where artists could create together.
